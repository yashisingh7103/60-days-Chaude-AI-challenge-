Day 3/60 – ABTalks Claude Challenge
Today, I explored Role-Based Prompting and learned how assigning a specific role to an AI can dramatically improve the quality of its responses.
💡 Key Learning:
Role-Based Prompting means telling the AI who it should act as before performing a task.
Examples of roles include:
✅ Developer
✅ Product Manager
✅ HR Manager
✅ Founder
✅ Marketer
Instead of writing:
"How can I improve user retention?"
A better prompt would be:
"You are a Growth Marketing Expert. Suggest strategies to improve user retention for a SaaS product."
The difference in response quality is significant. The AI provides more relevant, specialized, and actionable insights because it understands the perspective from which it should answer.

🎯 Takeaway:
The quality of AI-generated output improves when clear context and expertise are provided. Assigning the right role helps transform AI from a general assistant into a domain-specific expert.
